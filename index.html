A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good to know.

Still have a question? Write us a note ↗

How does a custom order work?＋

Email us with your idea and reference photo. We’ll discuss the right format, confirm your personal estimate, and begin after you’re happy to proceed. You’ll see a preview before the final artwork is prepared.

How much does a piece cost?＋

Each piece is quoted individually based on the product, number of people or photos, detail, and delivery needs. Email us your idea to discuss the options.

How long will it take?＋

Timing depends on the artwork and delivery. We’ll share an estimated timeline with your quote, and you can tell us if there’s a special date coming up.

Can I request changes?＋

Yes. Your preview is the moment to share the adjustments that will make it feel right. We’ll explain the revision plan before we start.
